Graphics settings

The in-game Settings → Graphics → Apply is broken in this browser port (freezes or refuses to save; reported by other users too). Set graphics here and launch — the engine receives them as launch arguments.

Note: changing preset needs a relaunch. Do not use the in-game Apply button. Saves are safe — this only changes launch arguments.

1 · Low / smooth

1024x576, 30 FPS, lowest quality. ~30 FPS on this laptop.

2 · High (optimized)

960x540, max textures, shadows at 0, reflections 1, no particles/grass. Measured ~16.5 FPS.

3 · Max

1280x720, every quality setting max. ~7 FPS.

4 · Max + HD

Same as Max plus the sharpen filter so 720p looks closer to 1080p.

5 · Mobile touch

On-screen buttons + swipe look. Phone needs Chrome 133+ / WebGPU + Memory64 and HTTPS.

6 · High graphics

1024x540, max textures/shadows/reflections, 50% density. ~7-9 FPS on this laptop.



Expected: Low ~30 FPS · Medium ~13 FPS · Max ~7 FPS on i3-7100U / HD 620. These are measured, not promises.
